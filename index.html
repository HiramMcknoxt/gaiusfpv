Song requests — open

Request a song. I'll fly it.

Every flight is one full song, one take. If there's a song you want to see me fly, send it here. Good requests get added to the public request queue on Spotify, and if I fly yours, you get the shoutout you leave in the form.

How it works

1. You request it
Song title + artist. A Spotify link helps, and your name / @handle gets you a shoutout.
2. I check it
One rule: I fly every song once. If I've already posted a flight to it, it's skipped — check the list below before you ask.
3. It joins the queue
Accepted songs land on the GaiusFPV Request Playlist on Spotify.
4. I fly it
Full song, one take, posted to YouTube, TikTok and Facebook as GaiusFPV.

No account or email needed to submit. Requests are checked regularly and added to the playlist in batches.

About the flying

Lyrical freestyle means the tricks are timed to the music — rolls, flips and dives landing on beats, drops and lyric cues, for the full length of the song, in one take. For piloted footage, editing may select honest moments; it never re-times a flight to manufacture sync it didn't have.

Looking for calm aerial cinematography instead — landscapes, reveals, orbits, real-estate style work? That's my other channel, Ataraxia Aerials. GaiusFPV is the aerobatics side; Ataraxia is the scenic side.

Want to fly along? Try Drone Hero — a browser FPV game built from my own flying spots.
